How to calibrate
  1. Open the camera in the Webcam tab.
  2. Open the calibration grid on the physical display and enter fullscreen.
  3. Align the centre targets, then scan all nine shifted patterns.
  4. Calculate calibration and inspect the straightness of the preview grid.

The alignment board returns automatically after the fixed-display scan.
