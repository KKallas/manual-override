# Kling VIDEO 3.0 turret-install prompts

Use **Start & End Frames-to-Video**, set the duration to **3 seconds**, and keep the output at **1:1**. Upload each matching `first` image as the start frame and `last` image as the end frame. Generate one turret at a time.

## Shared continuity rules

These constraints are already included in every prompt below and should remain unchanged:

- One continuous, fixed, direct-overhead orthographic shot.
- The square installation stays locked to the exact image center and fixed center pivot.
- No camera movement, zoom, shake, tilt, parallax, reframing, cropping, or cuts.
- No translation, scaling, footprint changes, design morphing, duplicate parts, scenery, ground, text, UI, people, ammunition, smoke clouds, or debris outside the square base.
- Preserve the isolated transparent/empty background and the crisp top-down strategy-game sprite style.
- The final six frames must settle without movement and match the supplied end frame exactly in silhouette, scale, position, orientation, palette, lighting, and mechanical details.
